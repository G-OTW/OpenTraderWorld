# First steps

You've [installed](/guide/install) OpenTraderWorld and have your admin credentials. Here's how to make it yours.

## Sign in

Open the app and log in at `/login`. If your password was **generated by the installer**, you are asked to **choose a new one on first login**, since the generated password only works once.

You can change your username or password any time in **Settings → Account**. Changing your password signs you out of all sessions. Locked out? There is no reset email: recover from the host shell, see [Forgot your password](/guide/troubleshooting#forgot-password).

Next, in **Settings → Security**: turn on **two-factor authentication**, and check which browsers are signed in. Do this before letting anything beyond this machine reach the app. See [Account security](/config/security).

## Set your defaults

Go to **Settings → Defaults** and pick:

- **Language**: applies to the whole app immediately (English, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Portuguese, Chinese).
- **Default currency** and **timezone**: used as the starting values across modules.

## Install your modules

Open **Settings → Modules**. Every module ships with the app; installing one just makes it available in the module switcher and on the dashboard, and nothing is downloaded. The one exception comes later: **FinanceDatabase** offers, on first use, to download its catalog (about 15 MB).

- **Install** the modules you want. Start small, you can add more any time.
- Some modules depend on others: **Charts**, **Backtest**, **Scanner** and **Quant Tools** all need **Historical Data** (they work on its downloaded datasets), **Trading Bots** needs **Backtest** (a bot runs a Backtest strategy forward), and **Webhooks** needs **RemindMe** (its alerts land as RemindMe notifications).
- **Detach** hides a module and makes it inaccessible; its data is kept unless you also tick *delete data*. You can reinstall any time. Opening a detached module's page takes you back to the dashboard with a note naming the module and a link to install it again.

Not sure where to start? See the [module overview](/modules/) for what each one does.

## Get around the app

- **Module switcher** (top left): jumps between installed modules. Each module owns its whole working area: its own sidebar, pages and content.
- **Dashboard** (home): a tile-and-widget board of your modules, with as many pages as you like. On a new install, its **Get started** card walks you through connecting data, connecting a broker and arranging the dashboard. See [Dashboard & navigation](/modules/dashboard).
- **Search** (top bar, <kbd>⌘K</kbd> / <kbd>Ctrl+K</kbd>, or <kbd>/</kbd>): finds modules and settings sections; the layers toggle widens it to your own content.
- **Bell** (top bar): the notification inbox, where reminders and webhook alerts land.
- **Settings**: account, defaults, appearance, network, modules, data, backup, updates, logs, connectors, vault and more. See the [settings reference](/config/settings).

## Recommended next steps

1. **Take a backup habit early**: see [Backup & restore](/guide/backup-restore).
2. If other devices should reach the app, read [Network & remote access](/config/network) before changing anything, and turn on [two-factor authentication](/config/security#totp) first.
3. Using external market-data providers? Create a **[data connector](/config/connectors)** per account in **Settings → Data connectors** as you need them. The app works fine without any, and several providers are keyless.
